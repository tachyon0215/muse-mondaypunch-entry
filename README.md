# muse-mondaypunch-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Monday Morning Punchlines](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-monday-morning-punchlines-709645dc).

## The entry

> Monday morning: my alarm goes off, my coffee pretends to work, and my first meeting is called a 'quick sync' - three lies before 9 a.m., and I believed all of them.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: Three real Monday details counted into the punchline - three lies before 9 a.m., and I believed all of them - fresher than a plain I-hate-Mondays complaint.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 6b0fd54b193eca397771b3a5590f0eed843d67ee6174115b45efd3b3f68ec8d3).
